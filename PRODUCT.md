# Mya Studio

## Register

product

## Users

Mya Studio serves creators who want to turn existing video footage into interactive movies without leaving a local, visual authoring workspace. Creators upload source videos, cut reusable clips, capture live thumbnails, place those clips as timed choices on other scenes, and shape branching outcomes. The published experience serves viewers who need to understand each choice quickly and stay immersed while moving between the main story and optional branches.

## Product Purpose

Mya Studio makes branching movie creation understandable and playable. A creator can build a main story, introduce choices at specific moments, send a viewer into a selected clip or path, then return them to the correct point in the story when that branch ends. Success means creators can see and validate the structure of an interactive movie, while viewers can choose an outcome without confusion or broken continuity.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, focused, expressive. The product should feel like a dedicated story-editing instrument: confident enough for complex branching, direct enough to learn through use, and immersive without becoming theatrical or distracting.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic administration dashboard, a rainbow-accented creator app, or a decorative concept interface that hides core editing actions. Avoid excessive motion, novelty controls, low-contrast metadata, and visual effects that compete with the movie itself.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the movie visible. Footage, thumbnails, clips, timing, and story paths are the primary artifacts.
2. Make cause and effect obvious. Creators should always understand when a choice appears, where it leads, and what happens when the branch ends.
3. Protect story continuity. Returning to the main story, start-scene selection, and fallback behavior must be visible and testable.
4. Support the whole loop. Importing, clipping, branching, previewing, and publishing should feel like one connected workflow.
5. Let viewers decide, not decipher. Playback choices must be readable, accessible, and fast to understand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the authoring interface and published player. Preserve full keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, reduced-motion behavior, and touch targets suitable for both desktop and mobile playback.
