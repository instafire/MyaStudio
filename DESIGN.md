# Mya Studio Design System

## Direction
Mya Studio should feel like a cinematic authoring tool, not a generic dashboard. The UI combines:

- a dark product-forward shell inspired by design-tool sites with pure-black backgrounds, compressed display typography, and a restrained electric-blue accent
- a builder workspace that behaves like a visual map, with strong containment, clear scene cards, and obvious graph actions

The app should present the movie itself as the hero artifact. Screenshots, thumbnails, clips, and branches should feel like the product.

## Theme
- Background: pure black and near-black only
- Accent: one primary blue for interactive states
- Supporting semantics:
  - soft gold for warnings and return-path callouts
  - soft green for valid start/completed states

## Typography
- Display/headings: `Space Grotesk`, tight tracking, large contrast
- Body/UI: `Inter`
- Labels, counters, metadata: `Azeret Mono`

## Components
- Primary buttons are white pill buttons
- Secondary buttons are dark frosted pills
- Panels are dark layered surfaces with subtle blue ring containment
- Inputs are rounded dark controls with blue focus rings
- Scene nodes are elevated cards with thumbnail-first composition

## Layout
- Spacious between sections, dense inside tools
- Large rounded shells for major work areas
- Builder and logic surfaces should feel like dedicated workspaces, not form pages

## Do
- Keep the interface product-centric and cinematic
- Use the blue accent sparingly but consistently
- Prefer strong hierarchy and fewer visual colors
- Let thumbnails and branching structure do the visual storytelling

## Do Not
- Reintroduce rainbow accents or purple-heavy gradients
- Use flat gray admin-dashboard styling
- Mix too many button shapes
- Hide important story status behind low-contrast text
