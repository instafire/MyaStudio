How it works

Three steps to an interactive movie

From raw footage to a fully playable branching story in minutes. No coding required.

01 🎬

Import your footage

Upload video or audio files (up to 800 MB each). Trim scenes, adjust speed, apply filters, and set background music per clip.

02 🔀

Branch the story

Use the visual graph editor to connect scenes with choices. Add variables, conditions, and time-based triggers. Test the flow live.

03 🚀

Publish & share

Export a self-contained HTML player. Share the link, embed it on any page, or showcase it in the interactive feed. Works offline.

Features

Everything you need to create

A complete suite of tools for crafting interactive video stories, from clip editing to analytics.

🌐

Visual Graph Builder

Drag-and-drop node editor with auto-layout, zoom, pan, and SVG connections.

Connect any scene with choices. See your entire story as a visual map. Double-click to rename, drag to reconnect.
⏱️

Timeline Logic

Place choice blocks at precise timestamps on a visual timeline per scene.

Each block supports up to 4 choices with custom labels, colors, and target actions — including return-to-main and end-movie.
🧩

Choice Variables

Add game-state logic with set/require conditions. Lock choices behind variables.

Set a variable when a choice is made. Require it to unlock other choices. Build complex branching narratives with state memory.
📦

Standalone Export

Generate a self-contained HTML player with all assets inlined or copied.

The exported player works offline, on any device. Includes save/load, choice preview on hover, and background music.
📱

Interactive Feed

Published movies appear in a swipeable vertical feed with like/view tracking.

Each published movie gets its own tile in the feed. Viewers swipe through, see metrics, and jump into the interactive player.
📊

Choice Analytics

Track how viewers navigate your story with per-choice click statistics.

See which choices are most popular, identify drop-off points, and refine your story based on real viewer behavior.

Deep dive

The Builder — your story on a canvas

Every scene is a node. Every choice is a connection. Drag to arrange, double-click to edit, connect to branch. The graph is your story.

  • ✓ Auto-layout organizes your graph into readable levels
  • ✓ Dead-end detection highlights unreachable scenes
  • ✓ Live preview lets you test any scene before publishing
