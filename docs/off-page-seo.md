# Off-Page SEO Strategy — Content Only

No outreach has been done. No emails sent, no submissions made, no accounts
created. This document is the asset brief and the plan; outreach is a
separate, human-approved step.

## 5.1 Backlink asset: "Free Interactive Video Maker"

### The asset
The landing page itself (`/interactive-video-maker`) is the linkable asset:
a free, open-source interactive video maker with a live app, a published
feed of example movies, and standalone HTML export. The pitch for any
roundup or list is one sentence: "Mya Studio is a free, open-source tool
for making branching, choose-your-own-adventure videos — no account, no
subscription, exports to plain HTML."

### Outreach targets (to contact only with approval)
- **eLearning Industry** (elearningindustry.com) — "best free eLearning
  tools" / interactive video roundups
- **Creative Bloq** (creativebloq.com) — free video tools roundups
- **GitHub Awesome Lists** — awesome-selfhosted, awesome-video
  (list PRs, not cold email)
- **Product Hunt** — launch page
- **Hacker News** — "Show HN" post

### Anchor text strategy
- Branded: "Mya Studio" (default for most placements)
- Natural / keyword: "open source interactive video maker",
  "free branching video creator" (for roundups where the context is the
  category, not the brand)
- Never: exact-match "interactive video maker" as anchor on low-quality
  placements; keep the anchor profile mostly branded.

## 5.2 Digital PR: open-source launch angle

### Angle
"A free, open-source alternative to expensive interactive video platforms."
The contrast is the story: hosted interactive-video tools charge per
project or per viewer; Mya Studio charges nothing, runs locally, and the
export is yours.

### Story assets to prepare (not yet produced)
- A 1-minute demo video showing the graph builder in action: import →
  connect two scenes with a choice → live preview → export.
- 3 screenshots: graph editor, timeline choice blocks, exported player.
- One example movie published in the feed that a journalist can play
  without installing anything.

### Outlets (to contact only with approval)
TechCrunch, BetaNews, Hacker News, Reddit r/programming, r/opensource,
r/videography. Lead with the demo video, not a press release.

## 5.3 Community building

### Channels to create (with approval)
- **GitHub Discussions** on `instafire/MyaStudio` — support questions,
  feature requests, show-and-tell. This is the default home for the
  community because it needs no new account and lives next to the code.
- **Discord or Matrix** — only if GitHub Discussions shows sustained use
  (10+ active threads). Do not fragment the community on day one.

### Content loop
- Share "built with Mya Studio" showcases from early users: a short post
  per published movie, linking back to the feed entry and the creator.
- Encourage creators to blog their process; link to those posts from the
  showcase section. User-generated posts on personal blogs are the
  highest-quality backlinks this project can earn.
- Monthly roundup (in the feed or a Discussions post): the best new
  branching stories of the month.

### What not to do
No paid placements, no link exchanges, no automated outreach, no posting
the project in communities where it is off-topic. One genuine showcase
post beats ten drive-by links.
